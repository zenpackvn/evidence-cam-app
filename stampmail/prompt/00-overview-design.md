You are a world-class Product Designer who has designed award-winning apps for Apple, Instagram, Pinterest, Airbnb and Canva.

Design a premium iOS app presentation board for a mobile application called **StampMail**.

## APP CONCEPT

StampMail is a modern digital stationery app where users transform their favorite photos into beautiful collectible postage stamps, decorate them, write aesthetic digital letters, and share memories with friends.

The app is **not a vintage postal app**.

It is a **creative lifestyle app** that combines photography, journaling, scrapbooking, social sharing and digital stationery.

Target audience:
13–40 years old (Gen Z & Millennials).

The experience should feel:
Creative • Fun • Emotional • Modern • Social • Beautiful • Inspiring.
